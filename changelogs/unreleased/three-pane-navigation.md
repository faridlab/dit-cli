# Navigation in three parts

The labelled rail is now split the way VS Code splits its window:

- **An activity bar of icons** on the far left — Home, Docs, Morse, Work,
  Flow, Plan and Search, with Settings pinned to the bottom. Each icon's name,
  its shortcut and what it holds show on hover; the shortcuts are the ones the
  rail had, so ⌘1…⌘0 still go where they went. Work carries the Inbox count
  as a badge. Clicking the lit icon folds the side panel away and back.
- **A side panel with the sub-menu on top.** Work lists Board, Issues and the
  saved lists under it (Inbox, My issues, Starred) with their counts; Plan
  lists Timeline, Roadmap and Gantt. Every icon returns to the screen last open
  under it. The panel's width is dragged from its edge and remembered.
- **The view's own sections split below**, each folding from its heading and
  resized by dragging the line between two — Columns, Cards and Group by for
  the board; Filters, Context, Type and Saved views for issues; the flow's
  critical path, route and selection; the plan views' options. Folding and
  heights are remembered per section, in this browser only.
- **Docs gains two sections** under the page tree: the open page's **Outline**,
  which scrolls to a heading on click, and **Recently edited**.

Morse is unchanged: it keeps its own explorer and tabs, and the side panel
stays out of its way.
