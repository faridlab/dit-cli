# The rail says what each row is

The left rail listed eleven destinations as identical links, which hid that
they were three different kinds of thing:

- **Lenses** over one issue set — Board, Issues, Flow, Timeline, Roadmap,
  Gantt. Now grouped under **Work** and **Plan** by what the reader came to
  do. The old grouping put only half the lenses under a heading, so Board and
  Issues read as a different category from Timeline when they are not.
- **Saved queries** — Inbox, My issues and Starred are the issue list with a
  filter, and nothing else. They are drawn indented under Issues, quieter and
  without a shortcut, because presenting them as peers of Board invited the
  question "so which list is the real one?".
- **Material** — Home and Docs are not a view of issues at all, and sit
  outside both groups.

No route and no keyboard shortcut moved, so existing links keep working.
`⌘1`..`⌘8` are bound to the rail order by index, and a test now pins the two
together: a row that moved without the shortcut table moving would send the
reader somewhere they did not ask for.
